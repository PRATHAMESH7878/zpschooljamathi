About Us

Our School

P.M Shri Z.P Marathi School, Jamathi is a government school managed by the Zilla Parishad and recognised under the PM SHRI scheme of the Government of India. The school serves the children of Jamathi.

PM SHRI

Pradhan Mantri Schools for Rising India

PM SHRI is a Government of India scheme under the Ministry of Education. It develops selected schools into exemplar institutions that show how the National Education Policy 2020 is put into practice.

Focus areas of the scheme

Quality Teaching and Learning

Competency-based teaching that builds understanding and not only memory.

Inclusive and Safe School

A welcoming environment where every child can attend, take part and learn.

Smart Classrooms

Digital tools and technology that support everyday lessons.

Green and Sustainable Campus

Care for the environment through clean, green and energy-aware practices.
